Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what Studio Lorenzo ("we", "us") collects when you visit lorenzodigital.com, why, and the choices you have. We keep it deliberately short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Anonymous visit counts. We record a simple daily total of visits so the studio can see how many people view the site. This count is not linked to your name, email address or device.
  • Information you send us. If you contact us by phone, email or social media, we use what you share only to reply to you and to discuss a booking.

Local storage and cookies

The site stores small items in your browser, such as a cached copy of the gallery so pages load faster, a flag that stops the same visit being counted twice in one session, and your choice on the cookie notice. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase hosts our database, photographs and the studio's sign-in system.
  • Vercel hosts the website and may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type for security and delivery.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces; your browser requests them from Google's servers.

These providers have their own privacy policies and process data under their own terms.

Photographs and models

Photographs and model profiles shown on this site are published with the agreement of the people involved. If you appear on the site and would like an image or profile changed or removed, contact us and we will act on it promptly.

Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it except with the service providers above, or where the law requires it.

Your choices

You may ask what information we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or withdraw consent for anything you previously sent us.

Contact

Use the details in the Contact section of the site for any privacy question or request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
